Project Atlas is a distributed analytics platform used by several internal teams.

The ingestion layer receives CSV, JSON, and streaming telemetry data. Files are validated, normalized, and routed into storage buckets.

The compute layer runs batch transforms every hour and can also launch ad-hoc jobs for analysts.

The metadata service tracks ownership, retention rules, schemas, and audit events.

The user dashboard shows job status, data freshness, and alert history.

Many teams requested stronger role-based access control and better query logging.

The current encryption standard for archival backups is AES-256-GCM.

The support team reported slow exports during end-of-quarter reporting.

The next roadmap item is cross-region failover.
